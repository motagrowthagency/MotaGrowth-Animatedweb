01 / Baseline Context

The Starting Point

TG2D operates in construction, general works, and sustainable development, with activities that include structural works, civil engineering, technical installations, maintenance, and multi-trade coordination. Despite this operational foundation, the company had no real digital structure to communicate who it was, what it did, or why clients should trust it.

Before the project, TG2D’s digital side was essentially nonexistent. The company had no professional online storefront, no organized presentation of its services, and no reliable platform to support commercial discussions, partnerships, or project inquiries.

This created a clear gap between what TG2D could do on the ground and how it appeared to the market.

02 / Strategic Obstacles

The Challenge

The challenge was not simply to “build a website.” It was to transform TG2D from a company with no digital visibility into a firm that could present itself clearly, professionally, and credibly online—while also being equipped internally to manage the opportunities that digital visibility creates.

Key challenges included:

  • No structured digital identity or online positioning.
  • No clear presentation of services, expertise, or company values.
  • No professional platform to build trust with clients, partners, and suppliers.
  • Limited visual and content assets, requiring a strategy based on accuracy and credibility rather than exaggerated claims.
  • No internal system to manage leads, client requests, or commercial follow-up.
  • Market positioning: A need to position TG2D as a serious, modern construction and development company in a competitive Moroccan market.

The objective was to build a complete digital foundation: a public-facing presence and an internal structure that could support real business growth.

03 / Comprehensive Execution

Our Approach

We approached TG2D as a full digital transformation project, not just a website design task. The goal was to create a clean, credible, and scalable digital ecosystem aligned with the company’s construction and sustainable-development positioning.

Brand & Visual Direction

We developed a clean, architectural visual direction built around TG2D’s identity: white backgrounds, charcoal typography, generous spacing, and the company’s signature cyan as an accent. The logo’s building-line concept was used as inspiration for subtle design details throughout the interface, reinforcing a technical, modern, and reliable image.

“Des fondations solides pour un avenir meilleur” — TG2D Official Tagline preserved and integrated throughout the architecture.

The tone was intentionally professional: strong, clear, and grounded, designed to make TG2D look established, structured, and trustworthy.

Website Architecture

We structured the digital presence around the essential pages a construction company needs to communicate effectively:

Accueil

A clear first impression of TG2D, its engineering positioning, and core value.

À propos

Company identity, foundational mission, executive values, and approach.

Expertises

A structured technical presentation of civil engineering and works capabilities.

Flotte

Machinery, equipment assets, and heavy operational capacity.

Projets

A dedicated space for realizations, site milestones, and future case studies.

Contact

A direct, professional conversion point for commercial inquiries and RFQs.

This structure was designed to make TG2D easier to understand, easier to trust, and easier to contact.

Content & Credibility Strategy

Rather than filling the site with generic or unverified claims, the content strategy focused on building a credible foundation. We created a content framework covering the company’s services, positioning, and contact flow, while leaving space for TG2D to validate and supply real project information, photos, and operational details over time.

This approach protected the brand: TG2D could grow its digital presence without presenting information it could not yet support.

04 / Operational Infrastructure

The CRM: Building the Operational Backbone

Alongside the website, we developed a CRM system to give TG2D a real operational structure behind its digital presence. The CRM was designed to organize the company’s commercial and client-related activity in one place, turning scattered information into a managed process.

What the CRM Solves

Before the CRM, TG2D had no centralized system to manage leads, client requests, follow-ups, or commercial opportunities. Information could easily remain in messages, calls, or informal notes, making it difficult to track opportunities and maintain a professional response process.

The CRM gives TG2D a clear structure for:

  • Lead management: Centralizing incoming inquiries and potential clients.
  • Client follow-up: Tracking conversations, quotes, and project discussions.
  • Commercial pipeline: Organizing opportunities by status, from first contact to conversion.
  • Client data organization: Keeping contact details, company information, and project context in one place.
  • Team coordination: Creating a shared system instead of relying on individual memory or scattered messages.
  • Professional responsiveness: Making sure no lead or opportunity is lost due to poor follow-up.

Why It Matters for TG2D

For a construction and development company, the CRM is more than a technical tool—it is part of the company’s professional image. When a potential client contacts TG2D, the company can now manage the request in a structured way, follow up properly, and present itself as an organized and reliable partner.

This directly supports the company’s positioning: TG2D is not only present online, but also equipped internally to handle the interest its digital presence generates.
