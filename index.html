MotaGrowth developed a connected B2B website combining product discovery, quotation workflows, content, and customer administration.

700,000+ MAD

Reported lead value generated without paid advertising (Sept 1–Nov 6, 2026: 1.5k views · 243 new users · 1m 12s engagement).

What we delivered

  • Storefront: Redesigned presentation of ORSAP’s industrial offer.
  • Catalog-based commerce: Selection requests with quantities and calculated quotation totals.
  • Clear calls to action: Routes to browse, request a quote, or consult an expert.
  • Blog & documents: Dedicated content-management section.
  • Quotation management: Segregated general inquiries and product-based quotes.
  • Customer administration: Account records and centralized subscriber directory.
