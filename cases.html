PILLAR 01

From Brick-and-Mortar to B2B Digital Commerce

Legacy industrial procurement was slow, reliant on phone quotes, and restricted to offline relationship selling. MotaGrowth engineered an express digital outillage store with real-time MAD pricing, stock availability, and automated cart-to-quote conversion that enables procurement departments to place orders 24/7 with a 48h delivery guarantee.

PILLAR 02

15,000+ SKU Taxonomy & 300+ Brand Directory

Large B2B catalogs often fail due to confusing navigation. We architected a clean, 15-sector taxonomy (EPI, Heights, Handling, Fluids, Power Tools) paired with a searchable directory of 300+ global brands (3M, Makita, Bosch, DeWalt, ABB, Honeywell, Sika), establishing instant trust and rapid product discovery.

PILLAR 03

Multi-Tier Institutional Social Proof

Enterprise buyers don't buy from untested vendors. We engineered prominent client proof modules showcasing tier-1 corporate partnerships: BUTEC (equipping 1,200+ workers), Dislog Group (logistics hubs), SNEP (chemical industry), and Ventec, proving operational capacity for high-stakes tenders.

PILLAR 04

Organic Inbound SEO & Regulatory Authority

Instead of relying solely on paid ads, we integrated a technical HSE knowledge hub featuring compliance guides, safety regulations, and equipment selection criteria. This positions ORSAP as the authoritative technical partner in Morocco and captures high-intent organic corporate procurement searches.
