Legal Architecture

Privacy Statement & Data Ethics

Last Revised: January 2026 · Compliant with GDPR & Law 09-08

01 / Core Commitment

MotaGrowth operates on an explicit principle of data minimalism. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker client or visitor data. Any information transmitted through our inquiry forms, client portal, or direct communications is treated as strictly proprietary and confidential.

02 / Information Collected

When you submit a project inquiry or communicate with our studio, we collect strictly necessary details to evaluate your brief: your name, organization, direct email, WhatsApp number, and submitted scope details.

03 / Storage & Retention

Inquiry data is processed directly by studio directors and retained only for the duration required to prepare, deliver, and manage client engagements.

04 / Direct Contact

For data privacy inquiries or immediate deletion requests, contact our privacy officer directly at privacy@motagrowth.com.
